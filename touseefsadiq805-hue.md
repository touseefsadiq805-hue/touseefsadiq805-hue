## About Me

I am an Applied AI Researcher and Computational Engineer specializing in **Multimodal Machine Learning**, **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**, and **Cross-Modal Retrieval Systems** for intelligent transportation and critical infrastructure. 

I completed my doctoral dissertation (*"Engineering Multimodal Retrieval Systems for Urban Traffic Scenes: Architectures, Fusion, and Performance Trade-offs"*) at the **University of Agder (UiA)** within the **Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research (CAIR)** and **SFI NORCICS** (Norwegian Centre for Cybersecurity in Critical Sectors).

Prior to my doctoral work in Norway, I earned an **MSc in Communications and Computer Networks Engineering** from **Politecnico di Torino** (Italy) on a fully funded merit scholarship, and a **BSc in Computer Engineering** from **Bahria University** (Islamabad).

* **Current Focus:** Fine-tuning Vision-Language foundation models (LLaVA, CLIP), contrastive representation learning, and low-latency inference on distributed GPU clusters.
* **Affiliations:** Centre for Artificial Intelligence Research (CAIR) and SFI NORCICS (Task 3.4: Humanized Deep Learning and Big Data Analytics).
* **Pedagogy:** Completed 200 hours of formal, certified University Pedagogy training at UiA.
* **Engineering Approach:** Designing reproducible, high-throughput machine learning pipelines that balance empirical accuracy with real-world latency and memory constraints.

## Key Research & Applied Systems

### 1. Multimodal Cross-Modal Traffic Retrieval
* End-to-end vision-language framework linking continuous camera feeds with natural language queries for target vehicle re-identification.
* Incorporated YOLOv7 detection, DeepSort tracking, fine-tuned LLaVA visual-linguistic alignment, and FAISS indexing.
* Deployed on AWS A100 GPU nodes with reproducible PyTorch pipelines.

### 2. IoT Network Intrusion Detection & Feature Selection
* Formulated novel feature-ranking and dimensionality reduction techniques for high-throughput network telemetry.
* Developed ensemble voting classifiers achieving superior intrusion detection accuracy across benchmark IoT datasets (*Sensors, 2023*).

### 3. Bayesian-Optimized Predictive Modeling
* Designed Bayesian-optimized gradient boosted architectures (XGBoost) for medical and industrial dynamic time series (*PLOS ONE, 2025*).
* Evaluated uncertainty quantification and feature interpretability for high-stakes decision systems.

### 4. Critical Infrastructure Cyber-Physical Anomaly Detection
* Contributed to SFI NORCICS Task 3.4 on humanized deep learning, examining robust representations under adversarial noise and real-world sensor drift.
